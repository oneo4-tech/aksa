## D2. Structured

**How testers think when AI writes the code.**

AI can write tests, heal tests, and review code. It cannot be responsible for what they miss. This channel shows the human questions that find real bugs:

- **Spot the Bug**: a test passes, something is still wrong. Can you find it?
- **Testing AI systems**: prompt injection, sycophancy, consistency, agents with too much power.
- **Tools under test**: new AI testing features, tested like a tester would.
- **Concepts in minutes**: oracles, testing vs checking, coverage, boundaries.
- **The human element**: careers, leadership, and war stories from real projects.

Every claim is demonstrated on screen or cited with sources in the description. Narration uses a synthetic AI voice.

Concepts from Rapid Software Testing are credited to James Bach and Michael Bolton and explained in our own words; no endorsement implied.

New long-form video weekly, plus Shorts.
