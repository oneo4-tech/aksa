# PROTOTYPE: gate checklists, two depths

Pick a depth per gate: **Lean** (fast, 5 items) or **Full** (thorough). The agent prepares evidence for each item; the owner says yes or no.

## Gate 1: idea and angle

Lean
1. The tester insight is stated in one sentence.
2. It fits a pillar (AI testing, Spot the Bug, Concept, Human element).
3. Its angle differs from past videos (agent lists the 3 closest).
4. A demo can prove it.
5. Long-form, Short, or both.

Full adds
6. Search demand and competing videos checked (agent lists 5).
7. Learning outcome written for the viewer.
8. Shorts cut points planned.
9. Rights risks: third-party code, logos, product names.
10. Sponsor or course tie-in, if any.

## Gate 2: researched script and demo plan

Lean
1. Hook in the first 5 seconds, topic clear by 15.
2. Every claim is shown on screen or cited.
3. Demo runs from a clean checkout (agent attaches the run log).
4. Length within target (word count and estimated minutes).
5. No banned phrases.

Full adds
6. Read aloud once by the owner or listened to as a TTS draft.
7. Pronunciation list updated for new terms.
8. Slides storyboard: one idea per slide, code within line limits.
9. Accuracy reviewed against sources (agent quotes them).
10. Distinctness: not interchangeable with a past script (inauthentic content policy).
11. Shorts scripts drafted with their own hook.
12. Captions wording equals the script.

## Gate 3: final video

Lean
1. Watched end to end at 1x.
2. Loudness at target, no clipping (agent attaches measurement).
3. Captions present and in sync.
4. Thumbnail readable at small size; title and thumbnail match the content.
5. Disclosures set: altered/synthetic setting, paid promotion, music credit.

Full adds
6. Encoding per YouTube recommendations (H.264, AAC 48 kHz, frame rate as recorded).
7. Chapters valid (00:00 first, 3 or more, 10 seconds or longer each).
8. No private data on screen (notifications, tokens, paths, emails).
9. Rights: code licences, fonts (OFL), music licence, art generated by us.
10. Accessibility: contrast at least 4.5:1, colour never the only signal.
11. Shorts: safe zone clear, related video linked.
12. Monetization fit: original educational value, advertiser-friendly language.
