# Render video

## Inputs and prerequisites

- The owner's feedback rules: `agentic/skills/content/run-youtube/references/feedback-rules.md`, its rows for step `render-video`.
- The channel and `#<n>`, a content issue at stage `editing` (from `youtube-producer`). No channel or issue: ask the owner which.
- Written below: `C` for `uv run agentic/scripts/content_issue.py`, `R` for `uv run agentic/skills/content/render-video/scripts/render.py`, `A` for `uv run agentic/skills/content/render-video/scripts/assemble.py`, and `W` for the workspace `.projects/content/yt-<channel>/`.
- The channel workspace, checked with `uv run agentic/scripts/youtube_channel.py check <channel>`; exit 2: show its message and stop. Read the style guide sections `Visual style` (theme, fonts, contrast), `Slides and animation` (focus lines, fades, visual pace), `Screen capture` (terminal recordings), `Audio` (loudness, music bed), and `Captions` (long-form SRT, Short word highlight), and in `channel.yaml` the `name`, `palette`, `background` (optional: the backdrop `image`, such as `branding/backdrop.jpg`, its `opacity`, and `fit`, `cover` or the default `contain`, validated by the channel loader), `loudness`, and `music_db` that `R` and `A` apply.
- The script and narration: `W/videos/<n>/script.json` from `write-video-script`, and `W/videos/<n>/narration/NN.wav` for every scene with `narration/report.json` showing `passed: true`, from `narrate-video`. Missing or not passed: stop and route the video back to `narrate-video`.
- The demo, when the script has `code` or `terminal` scenes: `W/videos/<n>/demo.json` (the demo's folder, whose files `code` scenes show) and the VHS recordings `W/videos/<n>/demo-1.mp4` ... `demo-K.mp4`, one per `terminal` scene in script order, from `build-video-demo`'s `record.py` (an older single `demo.mp4` is played across the `terminal` scenes only when no `demo-K.mp4` exists; a Short that `plan-shorts` cuts gets the cut scenes' `demo-K.mp4` renumbered). `demo.json` missing for a `code` scene: route back to `build-video-demo`. Some `demo-K.mp4` missing, or a clip longer than its scene (exit 2): route back to `build-video-demo` to record that scene's section timed from the narration (`record.py` after `narrate-video`), or shorten it; or to `write-video-script` to lengthen its narration. No recording while `terminal` scenes exist: ask the owner whether to record it with `build-video-demo` or draw the terminal scenes from their `visual`; no answer: report blocked.
- The channel's music bed `W/music/bed.mp3`, supplied once by the owner (the `Audio` bed, such as a Lyria ambient clip); it loops under every video. Missing: ask the owner for it and report blocked.
- Google Chrome, `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe`, `whisper-cli` with its `base.en` model, and the Space Grotesk and Fira Code font files, as the README's Setup lists; missing: ask the owner to install them and report blocked. Fonts load only from those local files, never from the network.

## Procedure

1. **Check the stage:** `C status <n> --channel <channel>` must show stage `editing`; else stop and report the stage.
2. **Render the scenes:** `R <channel> <n>`. Per scene it times each on-screen element from the narration's whisper word timestamps, then either draws the scene from its kind's template in `references/` (`title.html`, `points.html`, `code.html`, `terminal.html`, `split.html`, `compare.html`, `kit.html`, `intro.html`, `repo.html`, `cta.html`, with the shared `references/scene.css` and `references/scene.js`), stepping its Web Animations frame by frame in Chrome and encoding the frames, or, for the K-th `terminal` scene, plays `demo-K.mp4` whole at its own speed, holding its last frame for a longer scene and never speeding it up. It writes `W/videos/<n>/render/scenes/NN.mp4`, prints and writes `render/scenes.json`, and writes `render/alignment.md`, the alignment report: per scene, each on-screen element with its cue, when its own words are heard, the lag, and the words said from its cue, elements that enter with the scene listed as `frame` and a played recording as `recording`. The fields of each kind, with examples, are in `scripts/render.py`'s docstring (Scene kinds):
   - `code` types its file in within `TYPE_MAX` seconds (the first scene, the cold open, shows it whole from its first frame), so no focus or mark lights before its code shows, and focuses the lines the narration names; its optional `marks` (`line`, `note`, `at`) instead focus each line at its `at` word with its note in a chip beside it.
   - `split` shows two files, each with its label and path, side by side (stacked on a Short), with optional `marks` inside each side, and an optional `hook` (2 to 8 words, a Short's curiosity hook) shown big over the split from the first frame until the narration's first sentence ends (at most 3 s), then fading as the panels come forward; `compare` builds two columns as the narration names each item.
   - `kit`, the "Take it to your agent" beat, reuses the `repo` card and its animation: every file shows dim, each lights up at its `at` word with its `use` in a callout beside it (below the card on a Short), only the current file fully lit, the link wipes in at `link_at` (default `description`), and the takeaways come last, from `takeaways_at`, as a short numbered list beside (below) the card. No rules snippet panel.
   - `intro`, the long-form hook before the challenge, shows who it is for as role cards, the activity as a large challenge line, and the stake as a callout, each as the narrator names it (at its leading word).
   - `repo` introduces the AI kit before it is used, as a GitHub-style repository card in the channel palette (drawn book, folder, and file glyphs and a plain `github.com` label, never a third-party logo): the card at the scene's start, its path as a breadcrumb, its files listing in as the narrator names them, and the full `https://github.com/<repo>/tree/main/<path>` link wiping in at its `link_at` word (default `description`; unheard: after the files).
   - `cta`, the last scene, shows its `actions` (`like`, `share`, `subscribe`, `notify`) as flat icons drawn by the template (a thumb up, a share arrow, a bell, and a subscribe pill in the palette's deep red with "Subscribe" in warm white; no YouTube logo), each entering as the narration names it (like, share, subscribe, notification or bell) and doing one short move: a press, one pulse, or one bell swing.
   - Every drawn scene keeps the palette background colour and, when `channel.yaml` has a `background`, shows that image still behind all content, embedded so nothing loads from a network: `contain` centres it, faint; `cover` fills the frame, cropped, under a scrim of the palette background computed so warm white text and the cyan accent keep 4.5:1 even over a white pixel at that opacity (formula in `scripts/render.py` `scrim`); panels stay opaque. An invalid `background` or a missing image is refused (exit 2).
   - On a Short every scene keeps its content above the caption band (the lower area from `CAPTION_TOP` in `scripts/render.py` down), where step 4 burns the captions.
   - Every on-screen element enters at its own words, never before: a heading at its leading key word as the narration says it ("Bug one: it mocks the coupon logic" at "bug"), else another of its key words; each listed item (point, title word, split panel by its label, compare item, intro role, activity, and stake, repo or kit file, takeaway, mark, cta action) at its leading key word heard after the item before it, else another of its key words, so a key word said earlier in the scene no longer brings it in. A compare's two column headings enter together, at the later of their cues. A heading or item none of whose words is heard is refused (exit 2, naming the scene, the item, and the fix: reword the item or the narration with `write-video-script`); an element still cued before its own words, a backstop, is refused too.
   - No drawn scene is blank for over a second: one with nothing shown within 1.0 s of its start is refused (exit 2, naming the scene); nothing is pulled early to fill it. The fix goes to `write-video-script`: open the narration on the heading's words, or add a filler or transition `title` scene, such as a question in the centre, before it.
   - Motion rule: medium and purposeful, timed to the narration's cues, never decoration. Each element enters with one short eased-out move (slide or scale of 8%, 400 ms), focus bands and links wipe in, callout notes slide from their line, compare columns grow round a divider that draws, earlier elements dim, and entrances start at least 150 ms apart so one element moves at a time besides the subtle camera drift. No bounce, spin, particles, or loop. Intensity is tuned only in `MOTION` in `scripts/render.py`.
   - Exit 0: every scene rendered. Exit 2: fix or route back the input it names (script to `write-video-script`, narration to `narrate-video`, demo files to `build-video-demo`, a missing brand image to `brand-channel`, a `background` field to the owner). Exit 1: report the tool error; a font or Chrome error names the README Setup step.
3. **Check the alignment and the clips:** read `render/alignment.md`: no element shows before its words, and any with a long lag (it waits after the narrator reaches it) or a terminal step whose output shows before or long after its sentence is a fault, sent back as below. Check the clips against `Visual style`, `Slides and animation`, and `Screen capture`: open each `render/scenes/NN.mp4` and check the text is legible and inside the frame; each point, focus line, mark note, compare item, intro role, activity, and stake, repo file and link, kit file with its use, kit link, takeaway, and cta icon appears as the narration names it; each heading appears as the narrator says its leading key word; a mark's note sits beside its line without covering code; the motion follows the motion rule (step 2); the picture changes at the pace `Slides and animation` sets; and each terminal clip shows each step as its scene's narration describes it, never before.
   - A terminal step out of time with its sentence: the fix goes to `build-video-demo` (one `# comment` step per sentence, each output its own step, re-recorded with `record.py`).
   - A scene whose picture or wording is wrong: the fix goes to `write-video-script` (its kind, `visual`, `code`, `marks`, sides, intro, repo, or kit fields, or scene split), then narrate and render again. A template or timing fault: propose it to the producer as an improvement for the retrospective; never edit the templates in a video run.
4. **Assemble:** `A <channel> <n>`. It joins the clips in order with each narration at its scene's `lead`, loops the music bed under the voice at `music_db`, normalises loudness in two passes to `loudness.lufs` and `loudness.true_peak`, and captions from the script's `say` words timed by whisper: a long-form gets `render/final.srt`, a Short burned captions of three words with the word being said highlighted, drawn from `references/captions.html`. It writes `W/videos/<n>/render/final.mp4` and prints a report whose `measured` is the loudness (`lufs`, `true_peak`) of the written `final.mp4`; `mix_before` is the mix before normalising.
   - Exit 0: assembled. Exit 2: fix or route back the input it names (scenes to step 2, narration to `narrate-video`, the music bed to the owner). Exit 1: report the tool error.
5. **Check the video** against `Audio` and `Captions`: play `render/final.mp4` and check the voice sits clearly over the bed, no clip or scene cut is audible, and the captions (the Short's burned words, or `final.srt` loaded beside the long-form) show the script's words in time with the voice. Wrong caption wording goes to `write-video-script`; a timing or level fault is proposed to the producer as in step 3.
6. **Report on the issue:** write a scratch file of the line `YouTube: edit`, then each scene's number, kind, source (`html` or `demo`), and seconds from `scenes.json`, the total length, the final file and its captions from `A`'s report, and any scene sent back, and post it with `gh issue comment <n> --body-file <file>`. Return to the producer with `render/final.mp4` (and `render/final.srt` for a long-form) and `render/alignment.md`.

## Output

The `YouTube: edit` comment's link, `render/final.mp4` with its size, length, and captions (`render/final.srt` or burned), `render/alignment.md`, and per scene its kind, source, and seconds. A refused channel, a stage mismatch, a missing or failed narration, a missing demo file or recording without the owner's answer, a missing music bed, or a missing tool or font is reported as blocked with the script's message.

## Constraints

- Every picture and caption comes from the script, the demo, and the channel's palette and name; never invent on-screen facts or text the script lacks. Captions show the script's real words, never the transcript's or the pronunciation respellings.
- Never edits `script.json`, `channel.yaml`, the style guide, the narration, the demo, or the music bed; changes go to the skill or person that owns each file. The templates, `scripts/render.py`, and `scripts/assemble.py` are components: changes to them are new work requests (D-038), never edits during a video run.
- Generated media stays out of git (the workspace ignores `*.mp4`, `*.mp3`); never commits workspace files to aksa.
- Never passes a gate, moves a stage, or edits labels or the body; `C` and `youtube-producer` own those (D-047).

## Done when

- `R <channel> <n>` exited 0: `W/videos/<n>/render/scenes/NN.mp4` exists for every scene, `render/scenes.json` lists each with its source and seconds, and `render/alignment.md` lists every element.
- `A <channel> <n>` exited 0: `W/videos/<n>/render/final.mp4` exists, with `render/final.srt` for a long-form.
- `render/alignment.md` was read and no element shows before its words or waits long after them, and the clips were checked against `Visual style`, `Slides and animation`, and `Screen capture`, the final video against `Audio` and `Captions`, and every scene sent back is named in the report.
- The `YouTube: edit` comment is on the issue, with no placeholder and no em dash.
